Features

Sab Kuch Ek Hi App Mein

BharatChat mein woh sab hai jo aapko chahiye — messaging se lekar payments tak.

Instant Messaging

Lightning-fast messages with read receipts, typing indicators, and rich media support. Text, photos, videos, documents — sab share karein!

Voice & Video Calls

Crystal clear HD voice and video calls. Group calls up to 32 people. Low bandwidth optimization for 2G/3G networks across India.

End-to-End Encryption

Military-grade encryption for all your messages, calls, and media. Your privacy is our priority. Koi bhi nahi padh sakta aapki chats!
